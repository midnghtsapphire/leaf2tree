# Brand Guidelines

## Brand summary

Leaf2Tree is calm, sharp, and launch-oriented. The brand should feel like structured momentum: taking something small, messy, or early and helping it grow into a stable market presence.

## Voice

- Clear over clever
- Grounded over hype-heavy
- Confident over loud
- Practical over abstract

## Messaging themes

- Ship to market
- From rough input to clear structure
- Buyer-first positioning
- Launch surfaces that create trust quickly

## Visual direction

- Dark, high-contrast interface with botanical-green accents
- Clean card layouts, generous spacing, and soft glass-style panels
- Rounded corners that keep the experience polished rather than harsh

## Primary palette

- Background: `#07110f`
- Panel: `rgba(10, 20, 18, 0.78)`
- Heading: `#f3fff8`
- Body text: `#d7ebe2`
- Muted text: `#9fbbb0`
- Accent: `#9dffc2`
- Secondary accent: `#6ea8ff`

## Typography

- Primary: Inter/system sans stack
- Headlines should stay compact and high-contrast
- Body copy should remain short, clear, and scannable

## UI rules

- Every CTA should clearly describe the next action
- Avoid cluttering sections with too many parallel choices
- Keep copy blocks short enough to scan on mobile
