# Go To Market

## Positioning

Leaf2Tree should be positioned as a focused S2M (ship to market) studio for founders and operators who have a promising concept but need a sharp website, clearer message, and launch-ready documentation.

## Ideal customer profile

- Solo founders and small teams launching a new product or service
- Agencies or studios packaging a new offer
- Operators who have momentum internally but no clean public-facing launch surface yet

## Market signals

1. HubSpot's 2025 Website Marketing Report says increasing website traffic is the top website priority for 60% of marketers, reinforcing that the website is still the main launch surface for discovery and conversion.
   Source: https://blog.hubspot.com/website/website-management-trends
2. LeanData's 2026 State of Go-To-Market Efficiency report emphasizes that GTM teams are restructuring around the real buyer journey, which supports a buyer-first message and conversion path.
   Source: https://www.leandata.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/LD-State-of-GTM-Efficiency-112425.pdf
3. PitchGrade's 2026 GTM playbook argues that many GTM failures are really audience and positioning failures, which aligns directly with Leaf2Tree's value proposition around turning raw input into a focused market story.
   Source: https://pitchgrade.com/blog/go-to-market-strategy-startups
4. Recent 2026 website trend reporting continues to highlight speed, clarity, and trust as core conversion drivers, which makes a polished first-touch website a practical launch investment rather than cosmetic work.
   Sources:
   - https://www.capitalnumbers.com/blog/website-development-trends-2026/
   - https://kanopi.com/blog/website-design-and-development-trends-for-2026/

## Core promise

> We take rough ideas, scattered notes, and half-built offers and turn them into a launch-ready surface that is easier to understand, easier to trust, and easier to ship.

## Offer ladder

### 1. Signal Sprint

Fast positioning and landing page work for teams that need the first public version now.

### 2. Offer Reset

Message architecture and package refinement for teams that have drifted into unclear positioning.

### 3. Ship to Market

A combined website + docs + launch support package for zero-to-one releases.

## Launch channels

- Founder-led organic posting on X, LinkedIn, and Bluesky
- Direct outreach to warm network contacts and prior clients
- Case-study-driven landing pages for SEO and AI-search discoverability
- Partner referrals from designers, developers, and operators who do not offer GTM packaging themselves

## Initial launch assets

- Homepage with clear S2M framing
- Short offer deck or pricing page
- Founder intro post explaining the Leaf2Tree process
- One case-study template showing before/after message clarity

## Success metrics

- Qualified discovery calls booked from the website
- Conversion rate from homepage CTA to inquiry action
- Time from project intake to first market-facing version
- Repeat work from offer expansion after the initial launch
